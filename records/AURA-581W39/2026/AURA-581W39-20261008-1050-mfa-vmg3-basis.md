## Summary

Clarified and confirmed the baseline calculation methodology for model floor areas (FLA / FLH / Modelled Floor Area MFA) for the energy model. The gross floor area schedule from architectural drawings is to be used rather than net floor area. Additionally, aligned the space inclusion rules with City of Vancouver VMG 3.0 requirements.

## Decision, assumption, or concern

- **Floor Area Schedule Basis**: The energy model's FLA/FLH is strictly based on the **GROSS FLOOR AREA** table at the top of Page 3 in `.\Drawings 201610\arch\2026-08-28_BP Progress Set.pdf` (do **not** use Net Floor Area).
- **MFA Scope (VMG 3.0 Compliance)**: Unconditioned and ancillary spaces such as the **Garbage Room**, **Electrical Room**, and **Storage** must be 100% included in the Modelled Floor Area (MFA).
- **Model Area Values (current drawing / model `Proposed V4.dsb`)**:
  - Building MFA: **1,582.9 m²**
  - FLA: **1,578.5 m² (16,991.24 ft²)**
  - Net Floor Area: **16,174.36 ft²** (not used for FLA)
  - Difference between MFA and FLA: **0.28%**

## Basis and evidence

- Architectural drawing: `.\Drawings 201610\arch\2026-08-28_BP Progress Set.pdf` (Page 3, top table: GROSS FLOOR AREA).
- Confirmed area figures (user): current drawing values for model `Proposed V4.dsb`.
- VMG 3.0 Rule / Requirement:
  - Clause: *"All other spaces shall be included."*
  - Unconditioned spaces adjacent to vehicle parking areas (e.g., separate bicycle storage rooms, mechanical rooms, elevator shafts, garbage rooms, electrical rooms, storage rooms) are required to be fully included in MFA.

## Follow-up

- Verify energy model geometry and zoning to ensure the gross area and all specified unconditioned spaces (garbage room, etc.) match the Page 3 GFA schedule.
