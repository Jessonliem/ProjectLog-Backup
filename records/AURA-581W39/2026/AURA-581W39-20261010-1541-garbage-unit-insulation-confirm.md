## Summary

The insulation of the partition between the ground-floor **garbage room** and the **adjacent
residential unit** still needs to be confirmed — its assembly, whether insulation is present, and
its performance. If the garbage room is unconditioned or held at a low temperature, an uninsulated
partition increases heat loss and heating demand for the adjacent unit.

**Resolved (2026-10-13).** The partition is assembly **04a** — a **two-layer 4" steel stud wall
(i.e. two layers of W4b)** with **Roxul acoustic insulation**, effective about **R15**. The garbage
room itself has sufficient exterior insulation (**R16**). A few small portions of the adjacent
residential unit are **W4b/c** with only half insulation (about **R7–R8**), but these areas are
small and do not matter. The concern is therefore resolved.
