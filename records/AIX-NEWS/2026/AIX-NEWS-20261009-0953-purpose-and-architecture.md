## Summary

The "AI × Engineering" newsletter is established as its own managed project (ID `AIX-NEWS`).
It was split out of `Inbox Ideas` and the folder moved to the workspace root:
`C:/EnergySim-Workspace/AI x Engineering Newsletter`. Its log lives at
`C:/EnergySim-Workspace/AI x Engineering Newsletter/ProjectLog`.

The newsletter is a serialized essay series. The current plan is the **Foundation Arc, Vol. 01–10**,
built around one causal chain: AI makes execution abundant, so the strategic problem shifts from
producing more work to building the capability to decide, learn, and reshape the work. It is
organized as four acts:

- **Act 1 — RELEASE (#01–#03):** the old economics break down; engineering attention and the
  utilization trap; releasing capacity and re-investing it.
- **Act 2 — BUILD (#04A–#06):** the capability bridge — Acquire (Buy/Outsource/Build), Localize
  (external capability ≠ local capacity), Develop (three support layers + 2–10–50+ ladder),
  Multiply (representation and the engineer as capability multiplier).
- **Act 3 — ABSORB (#07–#09):** the measurement gap and prisoner's dilemma, the real cost of
  adoption, and building transition (resonator) infrastructure.
- **Act 4 — REDEFINE (#10):** what humans value when execution is cheap — judgment, direction,
  responsibility, and agency.

A recurring through-line is the three levels **Individual → Team → Organization**, with AI as the
catalyst across all three. Working materials include published issues (#0–#3), later drafts
(#04–#06), planned outlines (#07–#10), "thought seeds" 01–08, and a `Next stage/` planning area
with the master architecture and series map.
